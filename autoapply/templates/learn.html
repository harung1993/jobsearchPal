    Free training while you're unemployed

    Your state's career center (American Job Centers, like MassHire in Massachusetts) can often pay for training in an in-demand skill through the federal WIOA program, and approved training can excuse or count toward your work search in many states. Ask at your next appointment, or find your center at CareerOneStop.

    Check with your state's unemployment agency before starting a course, so it doesn't affect your benefits.
