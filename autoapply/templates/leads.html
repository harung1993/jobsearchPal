How leads work

After each email sync, the top leads are looked up on the company's own job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable; no AI). Matches join the roles the daily run can tailor. Star a lead and the next run searches the web for it; Auto apply does that for one role right away and uses credits. Score is a rough fit from your titles and resume.
